Mourning Activities During Bereavement
======
During my time at the HRAF, I did an independent research project, under the supervision of Dr. Carol Ember, investigating how widows/widowers mourn for their spouses after their death cross-culturally. This project involved systematically analyzing ethnographic records from George Murdock’s Standard Cross-Cultural Sample via eHRAF World Cultures, where I developed a standardized coding procedure to build a structured dataset for comparative analysis. This project will become the foundation for my Integrative Project at Teachers College (masters thesis), where I will be expanding it to incorporate a mathematical framework to model the mourning period activities and their broader social outcomes. I plan on submitting my project for conference presentations, and later on peer-reviewed publication.

PTSD & Post-Combat Rituals
======
In my undergraduate, I did an Honors Thesis under Dr. Luke Glowacki, where I investigated the role of post-combat purification rituals in mitigating symptoms of PTSD. Supported by multiple competitive research grants, I built a cross-cultural database from ethnographic materials and used Bayesian statistical methods to identify patterns associated with psychological healing. This research has been presented at both regional and national conferences and is currently being prepared for publication.

Language & Emotions
======
I was a Project Coordinator at the Psycholinguistics Lab, advised by Dr. Catherine Caldwell-Harris, looking into the Foreign Language Effect on Chinese international students through storytelling. As Project Coordinator, I managed a team of undergraduate researchers, leading our student-run research project. Some of my notable works in the project included designing our lab experiment and protocols, coordinating weekly meeting with our students and PI, advising our stats team with the data analysis, and presenting at conferences and symposiums. Our lab project is undergoing publication soon.
